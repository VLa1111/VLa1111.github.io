CAMSCKF: A Multi-State Constraint Kalman Filter with Adaptive Multivariate Noise Parameters Clustering and Estimation for Visual-Inertial Odometry

Yiyang Tang, Hanxuan Zhang, Yichen Yu, Xiaofeng Li, Yulong Huang

IEEE/RSJ International Conference on Intelligent Robots and Systems (IROS) · 2025

Adaptive measurement clustering and per-cluster noise estimation for visual-inertial odometry in challenging outdoor environments.

  • Groups visual measurements by their innovations and updates a separate noise covariance for each cluster online.
  • Evaluated in 50 Monte Carlo runs and on four UZH-FPV outdoor sequences. Mean trajectory and orientation errors on the outdoor sequences were 0.978 m and 1.658°.
